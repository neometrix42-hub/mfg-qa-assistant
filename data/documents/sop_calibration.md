## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines the calibration requirements for coordinate measuring
machines (CMMs) used for dimensional inspection of production parts.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all bridge-type and articulated-arm CMMs used to release production
parts. It does not cover optical comparators or handheld gauges, which are
covered by SOP-015.

## 3. Calibration Requirements

### 3.1 Responsibility

The Quality Engineer owns the calibration schedule. The Metrology Technician
performs verification and records results in the calibration log.

### 3.2 Calibration Interval

Each CMM shall be calibrated at intervals not exceeding **12 months** by an
accredited external calibration provider.

An interim verification using a calibrated ball bar shall be performed **every
3 months**. Any CMM that has been relocated, subjected to impact, or has had a
probe head replaced shall be re-verified before further production use,
regardless of the position in its calibration cycle.

### 3.3 Acceptance Criteria

Volumetric length-measuring error shall not exceed the manufacturer's stated
MPE_E specification. A machine failing this criterion shall be tagged
out of service and a non-conformance raised per SOP-021.

## 4. Records

Calibration certificates shall be retained for a minimum of **3 years**.
Interim verification records shall be retained for **1 year**.
