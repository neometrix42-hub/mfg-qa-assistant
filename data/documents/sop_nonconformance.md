# SOP-021 Non-Conforming Material Handling

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how material found to be outside specification is
identified, segregated, dispositioned and recorded, so that non-conforming
product cannot reach a customer unnoticed.

## 2. Immediate Actions

### 2.1 Identification and Segregation

Any operator or inspector who finds a characteristic outside its specified
tolerance shall stop further processing of the affected item immediately.

The item shall be tagged with a red NCR tag bearing the part number, batch
number and the failed characteristic, and moved to the **quarantine** area
within one working shift. Quarantined material shall not be returned to the
production floor until a disposition has been recorded under section 4.

Where a batch is suspected, the entire batch shall be quarantined pending
containment, not only the individual item that failed.

### 2.2 Containment

The Quality Engineer shall determine the containment boundary: all material
produced since the last known-good inspection point. Where no such point can
be established, containment extends to the full production run.

Material already shipped that falls inside the containment boundary shall be
escalated to the Quality Manager within **24 hours**.

## 3. Investigation

### 3.1 Raising the NCR

A Non-Conformance Report shall be raised in the quality system within **2
working days** of segregation. The report shall record the measured value, the
specified nominal and tolerance, the measuring equipment used, and its
calibration status at the time of measurement.

### 3.2 Root Cause

For any non-conformance recurring three or more times in a rolling 90-day
window, a formal root cause analysis shall be performed using 5-Why or
fishbone method, and a corrective action raised.

## 4. Disposition

Disposition shall be recorded by the Quality Engineer as one of:

- **Use as is** — requires written customer concession for any characteristic
  designated as key or safety-critical
- **Rework** — returns the item to specification; requires re-inspection of the
  affected characteristic and any characteristic the rework could disturb
- **Repair** — brings the item to a usable but non-conforming condition;
  always requires customer concession
- **Scrap** — item is physically defaced and disposed of

No item shall leave quarantine without one of the above recorded against its
NCR number.

## 5. Records

NCRs and their dispositions shall be retained for **7 years**. Customer
concessions shall be retained for the life of the product plus 7 years.
