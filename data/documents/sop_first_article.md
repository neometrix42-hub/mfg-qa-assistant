# SOP-030 First Article Inspection

## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines when a First Article Inspection (FAI) is required and
what it must contain, to demonstrate that a production process reliably
produces parts conforming to the drawing.

## 2. When an FAI Is Required

A full FAI shall be performed for:

- The first production run of a new part number
- Any change to the drawing revision affecting a dimensional characteristic
- A change of manufacturing location, machine, or primary tooling
- A production gap exceeding **24 months** for that part number
- Resumption of production after a process change raised under SOP-021

A partial FAI, covering only the affected characteristics, is permitted where
the change is limited and the Quality Engineer has documented the rationale.

## 3. Requirements

### 3.1 Sample Selection

The FAI sample shall be taken from a production run using production tooling,
production material and the production process. Parts produced by prototype or
soft tooling are not acceptable as FAI samples.

A minimum of **3 consecutive parts** shall be measured. Where the process is
new, 5 parts are recommended.

### 3.2 Characteristics to Measure

Every dimensional characteristic on the drawing shall be measured and recorded,
including reference and basic dimensions. Each characteristic shall be assigned
a balloon number corresponding to the ballooned drawing.

Characteristics designated key or safety-critical shall additionally have their
measurement uncertainty recorded.

### 3.3 Measuring Equipment

All equipment used for FAI shall be within its calibration interval as defined
in SOP-014. The calibration certificate number shall be recorded against each
measurement.

Where a characteristic cannot be measured with available equipment, this shall
be recorded as an open item and escalated rather than omitted.

## 4. Acceptance

An FAI is accepted only when every measured characteristic is within tolerance
and no open items remain. A single characteristic outside tolerance renders the
FAI failed, and the part shall be handled per SOP-021.

Conditional acceptance is not permitted.

## 5. Records

FAI reports shall be retained for the life of the part number plus **7 years**
and shall be available to the customer on request.
